In-cab inspection

Sit in the driver's seat with the examiner. The camera goes to each in-cab item in the order you'd show it on the California vehicle inspection test, and tells you what it is, what to check, and what to say. It moves on by itself.

Air brake check, hands-on

Do the whole check yourself with the truck's real controls: start the engine, build air to governor cut-out, run the one-minute leak test, fan the brakes down to the low-air warning and the spring brakes, then the parking and service brake checks. The gauges move like the real thing. On the California test, getting the air brake check wrong is an automatic failure.

In-cab test

Like the real test. Part 1: the examiner points at each in-cab item and you say what you check. Part 2: you perform the air brake check on your own, with no hints, and say whether this truck passes. Some trucks have a real defect. You won't see any answers until the end.

Based on the California DMV Commercial Driver's License Handbook (Section 11M, Vehicle Inspection Test) for a Class A tractor-trailer with air brakes. Always use your own truck manufacturer's numbers when they differ.
